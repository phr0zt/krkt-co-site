ABOUT — KRKT Co. / CRICKETTE COMPANY — GATINEAU, QC
MANIFESTO

We don't sell aspiration. You're already the point.

KRKT Co. — the Crickette Company — is a Canadian house where luxury meets anarchy. Premium formulas, honest markup, and a door held open for everyone the beauty counter ignored.

THE FOUNDER

Jennifer. Krickette. Both.

KRKT Co. was founded by Jennifer — Krickette to the people who know her — Ontario-born and building the house from Gatineau, Québec.

She grew up in the alternative community: the back of the room, the black eyeliner, the scene that mainstream beauty photographed but never served. The counters sold aspiration to someone she wasn't. So she built a counter of her own.

Every piece is made to order in North America. Every price is set by what it costs, not what the label can get away with. Premium is a formula, not a price tag.
