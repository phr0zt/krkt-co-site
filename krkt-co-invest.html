INVEST — KRKT CO. / SERIES ONE: LUXANA
THE OPPORTUNITY

Luxury didn't invite us. We came anyway.

KRKT Co. is building the house that mainstream beauty never built: premium-formula beauty and skincare for the alternative community, at honest markup, direct to customer — and then everything else they wear, carry and collect.

THE THESIS
THS—01

An audience the counter ignored.

Goth, punk, and alternative customers are photographed by the beauty industry but not served by it. They buy loyally from brands that treat them as the point rather than the aesthetic. KRKT is built from inside that community, not marketed at it.

THS—02

Premium is a formula, not a price tag.

Luxury-grade formulation with honest markup is a structural position, not a promotion. Made in Canada, sold direct on Shopify — margin comes from cutting the counter, not the quality.

THS—03

Two categories, one checkout.

Skincare and makeup in a single house — care under the war paint. The Luxana Crate turns that pairing into a curated, recurring format: full-line value in one box, with subscription economics ahead on the roadmap.

THS—04

One mark, many houses.

Beauty is the wedge, not the ceiling. The same audience and the same mark extend into clothing, footwear, bags, toys, crates and subscriptions — each launched as its own house under KRKT Co.

WHAT CAPITAL BUILDS

Series One funding goes where the audience can feel it: first production runs of the Luxana line, brand photography that carries the crimson identity, and the launch of the Crate as a recurring format.

The Shopify storefront at krkt.shop is live and ready to sell — capital converts the pre-launch catalog into inventory on shelves.
